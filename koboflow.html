YOUR REAL ACCOUNTS
Enter each real account and its balance at the start of the baseline date — i.e. the closing balance of the day before, from your Opay or bank app. From then on balances move only with the entries you record. Sample data stays separate and is hidden from your live figures.
ACCOUNTS (real money only)
EARMARKS (optional)
An earmark sets money aside inside an account (e.g. Rent inside Opay). It is not extra money and is never added to totals.
PLANNING TARGETS (optional)
Leave blank if you have not decided. KoboFlow never fills these in for you.
Money you want to keep for restocking and running the pharmacy. The Director will not suggest spending below it. It is your own figure, not a calculated requirement, and it is applied once: only to what the Director says is free to allocate.
MONTHLY BUDGETS (optional)
A monthly limit for one spending category. Progress counts expenses and bills paid in that category this month.
WHERE DO QUICK-CAPTURE ENTRIES GO?
The quick-capture bar and older entries use built-in account names. Choose the real account each one means. Nothing is assumed.
ADD A BILL
A bill is a payment you must prepare for, such as rent or electricity. Saving it moves no money: only a payment you record does.
For a bill you already pay, enter the NEXT date it is due. Earlier months are not tracked.
Occurrences due before this date keep their amount.
RECORD BILL PAYMENT
This records money that has already left the account. Pay less than the full amount to record a part payment.
RECORD PAYMENT
EARLY SETTLEMENT
Enter the payoff figure the lender confirmed. KoboFlow does not assume early payment reduces interest or waives fees — only the lender's figure does.
AMEND SCHEDULE
Replace the unpaid instalments with the lender's revised schedule. Paid and part-paid instalments stay as they are. The old rows are kept in history.
CONFIRM FUNDS RECEIVED
This records the actual cash inflow. Loan proceeds are not income.
